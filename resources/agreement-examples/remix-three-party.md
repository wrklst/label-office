# Remix agreement between label, artist and remixer (the label commissions the remix)

REMIX AGREEMENT

between

{{ label.legal_name }}, {{ label.address }}, {{ label.register }}, represented by {{ label.signatories }}
(the "Label")

and

{{ artist.legal_name }}, professionally known as "{{ artist.name }}", {{ artist.address }}
(the "Artist")

and

{{ payee.legal_name }}, professionally known as "{{ payee.name }}", {{ payee.address }}
(the "Remixer")

Effective date: {{ effective_date }}

1. Subject

1.1 The Label holds the rights needed for this agreement in the sound recording "{{ work.original_title }}" by {{ work.artist }} (the "Original Recording").

1.2 The Artist is the performer of the Original Recording and agrees to the creation of the Remix and to its release and exploitation by the Label under this agreement.

1.3 The Label commissions the Remixer to create a remix of the Original Recording with the title "{{ work.title }}" (the "Remix").

2. Permission to remix

2.1 The Label grants the Remixer the non-exclusive, non-transferable right to use the Original Recording and the parts supplied by the Label (stems) for the sole purpose of creating the Remix.

2.2 The Remixer makes no other use of the Original Recording or the stems, passes them to no one else and deletes them on request once the Remix is delivered.

3. Delivery

3.1 The Remixer delivers: {{ deliverables }}

3.2 Delivery is due by {{ delivery_date }}, through the online portal of the Label.

3.3 The Label may ask for reasonable changes within 30 days of delivery. The Remix counts as accepted when the Label confirms acceptance in the portal or releases it.

4. Grant of rights

4.1 The Remixer grants the Label the exclusive right of use (ausschließliches Nutzungsrecht), unlimited in territory and for the duration of the statutory terms of protection, in the Remix and in everything the Remixer contributed to it. The grant covers the Remixer's rights as performing artist (§§ 73 et seq. UrhG), as producer of the sound recording (§ 85 UrhG) and, where the Remix is protected as an adaptation, the Remixer's copyright in the adaptation (§ 3 UrhG).

4.2 The grant covers all types of use known when this agreement is signed, in particular: reproduction and distribution on sound carriers of every kind; making available to the public for streaming and download; broadcasting; public performance; use in excerpts and in connection with images for the promotion of the Remix and of the Label; and technical changes needed for these uses. Synchronisation with films, advertising or games needs the consent of the Remixer in text form, which the Remixer will not withhold unreasonably.

4.3 Types of use that are unknown when this agreement is signed are not covered.

4.4 The Label may transfer these rights to third parties and grant sublicences, in particular to distributors and platforms.

4.5 Copyright itself is not transferable and stays with the Remixer. This agreement creates no employment and no "work made for hire".

5. Composition

5.1 This agreement grants the Remixer no share in the musical work and lyrics underlying the Original Recording.

5.2 Of the publishing income that arises from the Remix as a separate arrangement, if any, the Remixer is entitled to {{ publishing_share }} %. This is subject to the consent of the authors and publishers of the underlying work and to the rules of the collecting societies concerned. The Remixer registers no arrangement with a collecting society without that consent.

6. Remuneration

6.1 The Remixer receives {{ share }} % and the Artist receives {{ artist_share }} % of the Net Receipts from the Remix. The remainder stays with the Label.

6.2 "Net Receipts" are all amounts the Label actually receives from any exploitation of the Remix, less (a) value added tax and other taxes on these amounts, (b) fees and commissions kept or charged by distributors, platforms and payment services, and (c) documented third-party costs the Label paid specifically for the Remix, such as mastering, artwork, manufacturing and promotion.

6.3 The parties consider this remuneration adequate. Statutory claims of the Remixer to adequate remuneration and to further participation stay unaffected.

7. Accounting and payment

7.1 The Label accounts at least once per calendar year and makes the statements available to the Remixer in its online portal. Statements show the uses and the receipts they are based on.

7.2 A balance in favour of the Remixer is paid after the statement is published, to the account the Remixer names. The Label may carry forward a balance below 25 euros to the next statement.

7.3 Value added tax is paid in addition where it is owed and invoiced. The Label may withhold and pay to the authorities any tax it is obliged by law to withhold, in particular under § 50a EStG, and gives the Remixer proof of it. The Remixer tells the Label their country of tax residence and any change to it.

7.4 Once per calendar year the Remixer may have the accounts for the Remix examined by a person bound to professional secrecy, at the Remixer's own cost.

8. Credit

The Remixer is credited as "{{ payee.name }}" in the usual way wherever the Remix is released.

9. Warranties

9.1 The Remixer warrants that they are free to enter into this agreement, that their contribution is their own, and that it contains no recordings or works of third parties (samples) that have not been cleared in writing and disclosed to the Label before delivery.

9.2 The Remixer tells the Label before signing whether they are a member of a collecting society or bound to a publisher or another label.

9.3 Where the Remixer is responsible for a breach of these warranties, the Remixer holds the Label harmless from the resulting claims of third parties, including reasonable legal costs.

10. Release

10.1 The Label decides whether, when and how the Remix is released. It is not obliged to release it.

10.2 Statutory rights of the Remixer to revoke the grant for non-exercise stay unaffected.

11. General

11.1 The parties agree that this agreement may be concluded electronically and that signing it in the online portal of the Label is sufficient.

11.2 No oral side agreements exist. Changes and additions need text form (§ 126b BGB) to be valid; this also applies to a change of this clause.

11.3 This agreement is governed by German law, excluding its conflict of laws rules. German legal terms given in brackets decide how the English term is to be understood.

11.4 Where the law permits such an agreement, the courts at the seat of the Label have exclusive jurisdiction.

11.5 Should a provision of this agreement be or become invalid, the rest stays valid. The parties replace the invalid provision with a valid one that comes closest to its purpose.
