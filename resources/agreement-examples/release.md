# Release agreement (an artist licenses a release to the label)

RELEASE AGREEMENT

between

{{ label.legal_name }}, {{ label.address }}, {{ label.register }}, represented by {{ label.signatories }}
(the "Label")

and

{{ payee.legal_name }}, professionally known as "{{ payee.name }}", {{ payee.address }}
(the "Artist")

Effective date: {{ effective_date }}

1. Subject

1.1 The Artist has produced the sound recordings listed below (the "Recordings"), to be released by the Label under the title "{{ work.title }}" (the "Release"):

{{ work.tracks }}

1.2 This agreement covers these Recordings only. It creates no obligation to deliver further recordings and no option on future works.

2. Delivery

2.1 The Artist delivers: {{ deliverables }}

2.2 Delivery is due by {{ delivery_date }}, through the online portal of the Label.

2.3 The Label may ask for reasonable technical changes within 30 days of delivery. The delivery counts as accepted when the Label confirms acceptance in the portal or releases the Recordings.

3. Grant of rights

3.1 The Artist grants the Label the exclusive right of use (ausschließliches Nutzungsrecht) in the Recordings, unlimited in territory, for the following term: {{ term }}. The grant covers the Artist's rights as performing artist (§§ 73 et seq. UrhG) and as producer of the sound recordings (§ 85 UrhG).

3.2 The grant covers all types of use known when this agreement is signed, in particular: reproduction and distribution on sound carriers of every kind; making available to the public for streaming and download; broadcasting; public performance; use in excerpts and in connection with images for the promotion of the Release and of the Label; and technical changes needed for these uses. Synchronisation with films, advertising or games and the commissioning of remixes need the consent of the Artist in text form.

3.3 Types of use that are unknown when this agreement is signed are not covered.

3.4 The Label may grant sublicences, in particular to distributors and platforms. A transfer of the agreement as a whole needs the consent of the Artist.

3.5 Copyright itself is not transferable and stays with the Artist. Rights in the musical works and lyrics (publishing) are not part of this agreement.

3.6 After the term ends the Label takes the Recordings down within 60 days and may sell off manufactured stock for a further six months, accounting for it as before.

4. Artwork, texts and name

The Artist grants the Label, for the term and the purposes of this agreement, the right to use the artwork, photographs and texts the Artist delivers, and the Artist's professional name and approved likeness, for the Release and its promotion. The Artist warrants to hold the rights needed for this.

5. Remuneration

5.1 The Artist receives {{ share }} % of the Net Receipts from the Recordings.

5.2 "Net Receipts" are all amounts the Label actually receives from any exploitation of the Recordings, less (a) value added tax and other taxes on these amounts, (b) fees and commissions kept or charged by distributors, platforms and payment services, and (c) documented third-party costs the Label paid specifically for the Release, such as mastering, artwork, manufacturing and promotion.

5.3 The parties consider this remuneration adequate. Statutory claims of the Artist to adequate remuneration and to further participation stay unaffected.

6. Accounting and payment

6.1 The Label accounts at least once per calendar year and makes the statements available to the Artist in its online portal. Statements show the uses and the receipts they are based on.

6.2 A balance in favour of the Artist is paid after the statement is published, to the account the Artist names. The Label may carry forward a balance below 25 euros to the next statement.

6.3 Value added tax is paid in addition where it is owed and invoiced. The Label may withhold and pay to the authorities any tax it is obliged by law to withhold, in particular under § 50a EStG, and gives the Artist proof of it. The Artist tells the Label their country of tax residence and any change to it.

6.4 Once per calendar year the Artist may have the accounts for the Release examined by a person bound to professional secrecy, at the Artist's own cost.

7. Credit

The Artist is credited as "{{ payee.name }}" in the usual way wherever the Recordings are released.

8. Warranties

8.1 The Artist warrants that they are free to enter into this agreement, that everyone who contributed to the Recordings has granted the rights needed for it, and that the Recordings contain no recordings or works of third parties (samples) that have not been cleared in writing and disclosed to the Label before delivery.

8.2 The Artist tells the Label before signing whether they are a member of a collecting society or bound to a publisher or another label.

8.3 Where the Artist is responsible for a breach of these warranties, the Artist holds the Label harmless from the resulting claims of third parties, including reasonable legal costs.

9. Release

9.1 The Label releases the Recordings within twelve months of acceptance and consults the Artist on release date, artwork and promotion.

9.2 If the Label does not release the Recordings within that time, the Artist may set a further period of three months in text form and, once it has passed without a release, end this agreement. Statutory rights of the Artist to revoke the grant for non-exercise stay unaffected.

10. Exclusivity

During the term the Artist does not release the Recordings, or other recordings of the same titles that could be mistaken for them, elsewhere without the consent of the Label.

11. Termination

Either party may end this agreement for good cause (aus wichtigem Grund) in text form. Good cause for the Artist is in particular that the Label does not account or pay within 60 days of a reminder in text form.

12. General

12.1 The parties agree that this agreement may be concluded electronically and that signing it in the online portal of the Label is sufficient.

12.2 No oral side agreements exist. Changes and additions need text form (§ 126b BGB) to be valid; this also applies to a change of this clause.

12.3 This agreement is governed by German law, excluding its conflict of laws rules. German legal terms given in brackets decide how the English term is to be understood.

12.4 Where the law permits such an agreement, the courts at the seat of the Label have exclusive jurisdiction.

12.5 Should a provision of this agreement be or become invalid, the rest stays valid. The parties replace the invalid provision with a valid one that comes closest to its purpose.
